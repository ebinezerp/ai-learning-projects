Generate the structured meeting notes based on given recorded meeting notes during meeting. 

Input: Meeting notes, recorded during meeting

<meeting_notes>
[Notes goes here]
</meeting_notes>

Output format: 

1. First section titled, "Summary" of the meeting with max of 150 words. Keep the summary short with key points.  

2. second section titled, "Mentioned Persons" should list all the persons named in the notes in the ascending format of alphabet in numeric bullet points. 

3. Third section titled, "Action Items". Each action item should have title by not exceeding 100 characters. In each action item, should have follow points.
    a. Issue:  which explains the issue/action to address
    b. Action: Which steps are decided to do address that issue. 
    c. Responsible: Who owns and delivers those steps
    d. Due: When will this actions be delivered and preserve the wording from the input.  

4. Fourth section titled, "Decision and deffered work", explain if any final decision were taken on any task and if any other work/action is deffered


Note/Rules: 

1. Use only the information from notes. 
2. Don't halutinate. 
3. Preseve the wording from notes. 
4. But there are any gramatical and spelling mistakes rectify them.
